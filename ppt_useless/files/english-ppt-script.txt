🎨 Painter Profession — 4-Person Presentation Script
👤 Person 1 — Introduction + Types

Slides 1–2

“Good morning, respected teacher and my classmates.”
“Today, our group is going to talk about the profession of a painter.”
“A painter uses colors, brushes and imagination to create visual art on different surfaces, including canvas, walls and even digital screens.”
“Painting is not just a hobby. Painters can work in galleries, films, advertising and video games.”
“There are different types of painters, such as fine artists, portrait artists, landscape artists, mural artists, digital artists and industrial painters.”
“Each type has a different purpose and style.”

Handoff:

“Now, [Person 2's name] will explain the skills and tools that a painter needs.”

👤 Person 2 — Skills + Tools

Slides 3–4

“To become a good painter, creativity is very important because painters need to develop original ideas.”
“They also need patience, because painting often involves layering, drying and making small improvements.”
“Observation helps painters notice details like light, shadow and shape.”
“A good sense of color is also important for creating the right mood.”
“Painters use different tools depending on their work, such as brushes, canvas, oil paints, acrylics and watercolors.”
“Modern painters can also use graphic tablets and styluses to create digital artwork.”

Handoff:

“But painting is not only about creating beautiful pictures. [Person 3's name] will explain why painters are important and introduce some famous painters.”

👤 Person 3 — Importance + Famous Painters

Slides 5–6

“Painters play an important role in culture and history because paintings can preserve traditions, stories and important moments.”
“Art can also communicate emotions that are sometimes difficult to express with words.”
“Paintings are used for decoration, design, films, games and advertising.”
“There are many famous painters who have influenced the world of art.”
“Some examples are Leonardo da Vinci, Vincent van Gogh and Pablo Picasso.”
“From India, we have Raja Ravi Varma and M. F. Husain, who made major contributions to Indian art.”

Handoff:

“So, painting can be much more than creating artwork. [Person 4's name] will now talk about the career opportunities and some interesting facts about painting.”

👤 Person 4 — Careers + Fun Facts + Conclusion

Slides 7–10

“A painter's skills can lead to many different careers.”
“Painters can work in illustration, advertising, animation, gaming, murals, movies and digital art.”
“Now, let's look at some fun facts about painting.”
“The Mona Lisa has no visible eyebrows, which has contributed to its mystery.”
“Vincent van Gogh sold only one painting during his lifetime, although his works are extremely valuable today.”
“Some cave paintings are over 30,000 years old, showing how ancient painting is.”
“Many movies, animations and video games also begin with digital concept art.”
🎤 Final conclusion

“To conclude, painting is not simply about putting colors on a canvas. It is a profession that combines creativity, skill and imagination, and it has a place in many different industries.”

“As our final thought says, every masterpiece begins with a single brushstroke.”

“Thank you. Any questions?”

🔥 Presentation Trick

Don't make it sound like 4 people reading four separate essays.

Make it feel like one conversation:

Person 1: Introduces the topic
⬇️
Person 2: “So what does a painter actually need?”
⬇️
Person 3: “And why is painting important?”
⬇️
Person 4: “Where can these skills lead, and what makes painting interesting?”

That'll feel MUCH more natural in front of the class.

And keep the slides doing the visual work — you guys should explain the points rather than read every word on the screen. The PPT itself already gives you the key phrases, so the spoken script should add just enough explanation.
